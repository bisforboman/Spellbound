# Match 1 - Seat 2 (Hoarder)

Personality: Hoarder. Hold land, keep mana, spend only when it clearly pays.

Final scores: Seat 1 18, Seat 2 17, Seat 3 14. Seat 1 won.

What I tried: I took Wanderer (separate groups) and kept two groups all game. The 4 Golems I summoned in round 2 beat Seat 1's 11-Imp attack 12 to 11. Later I used the surviving Golems to take two of Seat 3's tiles (one was Warded, and Seat 1 joined that attack). In the last round I used Leyline and then an attack to clear a monster tile without joining my two groups.

Strong: defending at the right moment, and Golems as attackers once they existed. Leyline before attacks (spells resolve first) was a neat trick.

Pointless or costly: the 4 extra Golems in round 7 guarded against an attack that never came. They cost 7 mana upkeep for nothing. Golem upkeep against Imps that cost no upkeep is a real trade-off.

Confusing: Wanderer works against conquest, because taking an adjacent rival tile can join your groups. Attune ties (round 1) waste a turn. A Ward on a tile nobody attacks looked weak, but the bot cast one every round.

No refusals; all my actions were accepted.
