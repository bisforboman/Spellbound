# Match 3, Seat 2 (Warlord, trait Hexer)

Final: Seat 2 32, Seat 1 22, Seat 3 9. Seat 2 won.

What I tried: summoned 3 Golems on round 1 (plus a Blight), then attacked every round after that. I took 5 tiles: four from Seat 3 and one fought out against Seat 1 in a three-way battle. Scorch and Blight went alongside the attacks and filled Hexer's cap of 3 points.

Strong: Golems. Three of them won every fight against empty or lightly held tiles. Conquest points plus the land they added put Seat 2 well ahead. Scorching a defender just before the attack was also good.

Pointless: mana piled up late (47 by round 8). Attacking takes up the stance, so I couldn't summon more, and unspent mana scores nothing.

Surprising or confusing:
- Scorch on an empty tile is refused at commit ("nothing to burn"), even though spells resolve after summons. You can't pre-scorch a tile you expect to be reinforced.
- My round-6 Scorch was accepted while a Golem was there, then burned nothing because the Golem had moved away. It still counted toward Hexer.
- Scorch can only burn up to the power present at commit time.
- In round 8 both Seat 1 and Seat 2 scorched each other's lone Golem, and both attacks fizzled.
- The `check` preview ignores your own Scorch ("keeping 2 Golems").
