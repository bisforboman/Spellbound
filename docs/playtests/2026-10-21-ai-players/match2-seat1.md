# Match 2, Seat 1 (Spellcaster, trait Warden)

Final: Seat 2 23, Seat 1 22, Seat 3 16. Seat 2 won on its trait (Hearthkeeper +3 against Warden +1).

What Seat 1 did: cast a spell all 8 rounds. Blight once, scorch 5 times, lure once, ward once. Leyline never: every time it could have helped, an ordinary free move to a neighbouring tile did the same job. Seat 1 passed or bid cheaply in the auctions, took the free leftover tiles and kept mana for spells.

Strong: scorch. Spells resolve before attacks, so scorching the stack next door wiped out three attacks before they landed (round 2: 4 Imps; round 3: 2 Golems; round 8: 5 Imps). At 2 mana per power it beats summoning defenders, because you don't have to guess which tile they'll hit.

Weak or pointless: lure (3 mana for -1 guard, and Seat 2 took the other monster tile). Ward only covers one tile when the rival can choose from three.

Surprises: in round 7, Seat 1 scorched the tile it was attacking for 10 mana, but Seat 2 had moved 6 Imps out first, so only 4 burned. Seat 2's scorch also burned Seat 1's attacking Imps before the battle. `check`'s "you lose everything you sent" leaves the same-turn scorch out of the forecast, which was confusing. Warden lost its points to plain conquest of undefended tiles.
