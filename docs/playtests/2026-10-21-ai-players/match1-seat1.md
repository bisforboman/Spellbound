# Match 1, Seat 1 (Warlord)

Final scores: Seat 1 18 (won), Seat 2 17, Seat 3 14. Seat 1 took the Weaver trait, and it paid 2 points for a group of 6 tiles.

What I tried: I bid for tiles that touch each other in the auction. I summoned 11 Imps in round 1 and attacked Seat 2's Steady tile in round 2. Seat 2 summoned 4 Golems that same round, so I lost 11 against 12 and all my Imps died. After that I attuned one tile, then put 10 Imps on the border tile. Seat 3's Golems attacked it and were wiped out, 9 against 10. Late in the game I took an empty Seat 3 tile and used Lure to weaken a monster to power 2. In the last round I cleared that monster with 3 Imps.

Strong: Imps cost 1 mana per power and need no upkeep, which beats Golems over a short game. Lure is cheap insurance for clearing a monster. Taking empty rival tiles is worth a lot.

Pointless or confusing: a stack of units next to a rival shows them where you will attack, so they summon defenders the same round. An attack only works where the rival can't easily answer. Scorch was refused on an empty tile ("nothing to burn"), even though the rival could summon there before spells resolve. When Seat 2 and I both attacked the same tile, Seat 2 got it and my lone Imp counted for nothing.
