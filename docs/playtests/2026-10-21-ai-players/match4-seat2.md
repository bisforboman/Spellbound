# Match 4, Seat 2 (Spellcaster, trait Beastbane)

Final scores: Seat 1 24, Seat 2 21, Seat 3 15. Seat 1 won.

Seat 2 cast a spell all 8 rounds: blight, ward (x3), scorch (x2), lure (x2). Four of the five spells got used. Leyline never did (see below).

Strong: scorch. In round 3 it killed one of Seat 3's 3 Golems for 6 mana. That made their attack 6 against 7 Imps, and it failed. In round 7, 12 mana burned both remaining Golems, which cleared the way to take the (-2,0) monster tile. Lure + Imps worked well: with guards lured down to 2 or 1, cheap Imps cleared monsters (a tie takes a monster tile). Ward + summoned Imps held (-1,0) against 9 Golem power. Imps (1 mana per power, no upkeep) look better than Golems almost everywhere.

Pointless: Ward on an empty tile in round 2 (no threat came). Blight was minor.

Surprising refusal: in round 8 I tried to summon Imps and leyline them away in the same round. It was refused ("There aren't 2 Imps at (-1,0)"), because leyline is checked against the units on the tile before summons resolve, even though spells come after summons in the order. Seat 2 never had idle units at the right time, so leyline never got cast.

Confusing: a monster tile I clear counts as "conquest" (it banks a point) as well as counting for Beastbane. Seat 1's Hearthkeeper (Steady tiles) quietly earned 3 points.
