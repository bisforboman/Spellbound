# Match 3, Seat 1 (Trait-chaser)

Final: Seat 2 (Hexer) 32, Seat 1 (Beastbane) 22, Seat 3 (Weaver) 9.

I picked Beastbane over Warbrand because monsters don't fight back. I attuned (1,-2) next to the first monster, summoned 3 Golems there and cast Lure on it. One Golem then cleared it (3 vs 2). For the second monster I cast Leyline and attacked in the same round, because Leyline resolves before attacks. That cleared both monsters, which were the only two on the map, so the trait could pay 2 of its 3 points.

Strong: Lure plus a single Golem is a cheap, safe clear. Leyline-then-attack in one round surprised me in a good way. Hexer's Scorch and Blight spam scored 3 trait points with almost no risk.

Weak or confusing: I left tiles empty and lost two Steady tiles to Golem raids in round 3. Every tile I held was undefended whenever I attacked. In a three-way attack, my two Golems lost to Seat 2's two. I didn't know a "Steady" yield of 2 still gets blighted. With only 2 monsters on the map, Beastbane can never reach 3. In the last round, Seat 2 and Seat 1 scorched each other's lone attackers, so both attacks fizzled, which felt symmetric and pointless. One surprise refusal: I attacked from a tile I thought was adjacent and it wasn't (I misread the hex neighbours).
