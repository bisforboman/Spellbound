# Match 5, Seat 2 (Trait-chaser)

Final: Seat 2 36, Seat 3 19, Seat 1 13. Seat 2 won.

Seat 2 took Warbrand over Golemwright, because Golemwright meant paying upkeep for 9 Golems. In round 1 it summoned 12 Imps on a tile next to both rivals. From round 2 on it attacked an undefended rival tile every round, 7 captures in all, and hit the trait cap by round 4. Each capture also earned a conquest point and more income, so the lead snowballed. Scorch was strong: it fires after summons but before attacks, so 24 mana wiped Seat 1's 4 Golems before they could act. It also killed units waiting to attack Seat 2's tiles twice.

What felt off:
- Rivals almost never defended, so imps beat ward every time.
- Mana piled up (67 at the end) because there is only one stance per round. Spare mana had nothing to do.
- The trait cap of 3 was reached by mid-game.
- Traits were revealed when the rounds began, even though the rules say the goal is hidden.
- `check` on the round-4 attack said "you lose everything" because its projection ignored my own scorch on the same tile. The attack actually won.
- Scorch is refused on an empty tile at order time, so it can't be cast ahead of a rival's summons.
- The Wild tile bid tied at 3 and nobody got it, but the free leftover handed the tile to Seat 2 anyway.
