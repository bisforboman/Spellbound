# Match 4, Seat 1 (Hoarder)

Final: Seat 1 24, Seat 2 21, Seat 3 15. Seat 1 won.

Seat 1 took Hearthkeeper (Steady tiles). In the opening Seat 1 bid modestly (2-3). Two ties at the top handed Seat 1 the contested tile for free anyway, so tying cost nothing. That made a cheap tying bid look stronger than it should. Seat 1 ended the opening with 2 Steady tiles and 0 spent on one of them. After that Seat 1 attuned a third Steady (round 1 tied with Seat 2, round 2 retry won) and summoned only Imps (no upkeep). It cleared three monster tiles (4-10 Imps against power 3), each time keeping a defender back or warding home. In round 5, Seat 1 scorched Seat 2's 5 Imps that sat next to two of its tiles. That cost 10 mana. Only 2 burned because Seat 2 moved most of them away first. Pricey for little.

Strong: monster tiles (sure conquest point plus land), Imps over Golems, leyline + attack in one round.
Pointless: hoarding mana. Seat 1 ended with 34 unspent; it only breaks ties. Blight in the last round would do nothing.
Confusing: whether units summoned this round defend (they do) and whether scorch sees moved units (moves happen first, so it missed). No refusals.
