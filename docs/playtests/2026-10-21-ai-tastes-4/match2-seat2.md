# 4X fan (match 2, Seat 2)

**Final scores and my trait**
Seat 2 31 (won), Seat 1 26 (Weaver), Seat 3 26 (Warden). My trait was Spellweaver: 5 different spells, cast in 6 rounds, so I got the full 3 points.

**What I enjoyed**
- The Unveils. Dark tiles appearing in rounds 2 and 4, and a guarded Wild tile showing up right next to my land, was the closest thing to exploring.
- Clearing the monster with one Golem that I Leylined across my land (a tie takes the tile) paid 6 points in one round. It was the best moment of the match.
- Spells run after summons, so I could summon 7 Golems and Leyline 3 of them onto my other border tile in the same round. That split matched Seat 1's 9-power attack exactly and the fight was a tie.
- Scorching away all 3 of Seat 1's Golems in round 7 just as they Rallied against my tile felt like a real counter-play.

**What bored or frustrated me**
- Growth stops early. From round 4 an attune cost 10-13 mana, so I held 6 tiles from round 4 to the end. There was no long arc from small to mighty.
- One stance per round left mana unused. I banked 22 to 38 unspent mana in several rounds with nothing to put it into.
- There are only 3 upgrades and they are flat. A Shrine is +1 yield and does not grow into anything. There's no tech tree and nothing unlocks.
- Rounds 7-8 played like rounds 1-2 with bigger numbers. In the last round I just built a Shrine for its end point and gave up an empty tile.
- The map is 19 hexes, so it feels like a crowded border right away rather than a frontier.

**Did reading my rivals matter?**
Yes, more than anything else. I could see units only on tiles next to my land. I read Seat 1's 3 Golems next to my border and defended correctly. Then I guessed wrong: I attacked (0,1) with Rally while Seat 1 summoned 6 Golems there, and I lost my whole army. I guessed both traits wrong. I took Seat 3's two Blights to mean Hexer, but it was Warden. Seat 1's repeated Wards on (0,1) looked like Warden, but it was Weaver. Seat 3 guessed both of us right and took 2 points from it. My mistakes didn't cost me the win, though, because the monster kill and my trait gave me the lead.

**Did building and fighting feel worth it?**
Fighting did. Every battle shifted points or tempo, and ties make defending cheap. Building only felt worth it as a 5-mana purchase of 1 end point. A Tower or Shrine never changed how I played afterwards.

**Compared with the games I love, what's missing**
Compounding: an economy that grows into new capabilities. A real unknown to explore. Any sense of an empire changing shape over time.

**Top 3 additions I'd want**
1. A small tech or upgrade tree where Shrines unlock better spells or units, like Eclipse's research track.
2. A bigger map with more Unveils and exploration events (ruins, relics), like Twilight Imperium's frontier tokens.
3. More than one action per round as the realm grows (extra actions per tier), like Civilization's cities producing in parallel.

**Would I play again?** 3/5. The tactical mind-games are tight and quick. But it's a 20-minute skirmish, not the 4X arc I'm looking for.
