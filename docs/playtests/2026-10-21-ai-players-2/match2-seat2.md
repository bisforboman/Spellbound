# Match 2, Seat 2 (Trait-chaser)

Trait: Spellweaver. I picked it over Weaver because 9 connected tiles looked out of reach on a 19-tile map. Final score: Seat 1 31, Seat 2 27, Seat 3 15. Seat 1 won.

Plan: cast four different spells as cheaply as I could, then switch to points. I cast Blight in R1 (4 mana, a weak pick but it ticked the box), Lure in R2 (3, worth it: one Golem then cleared the monster for a tile and a point), Scorch in R3 (9 for 3 Imps, which blunted Seat 3's attack) and Ward in R4. That maxed the trait by round 4. Golems taking empty rival tiles earned me conquest points in R3 to R5.

Mistakes: in R6 I attacked a tile that Seat 3 had just filled with 12 summoned Imps, and lost both Golems. Seat 1's Scorch had already burned one of them before the attack. My R7 Scorch of those 12 Imps cost 36 mana. It saved my tiles but was very expensive. In R8, Seat 1 scorched all 8 of my Imps, so my monster attack never happened.

What felt strong: Scorch (it resolves before attacks, so it wipes out the attackers before they arrive) and Seat 1's huge mana bank. What felt pointless: Blight in the last round. What surprised me: Seat 1's first Scorch on my Golem burned nothing, I guess because the count was less than the Golem's power of 3. And summoned units can defend in the same round, so a tile with no units can still be held when you attack it.
