# Match 3, Seat 1: Rules-lawyer, trait Warbrand

Final scores: Seat 1 27, Seat 3 22, Seat 2 9. Seat 1 won, with 4 rival tiles taken and the trait at its 3-point cap.

What I tried:
- **Opening:** my bid tied, and I got the same tile free as a leftover anyway. I then passed both remaining auctions and still got free tiles. Passing every auction gave 3 tiles and all 9 mana kept. This looks like a loophole: bidding barely pays.
- **First attack failed:** my 8 Imps lost to 9 Imps summoned on defense that same round. Defenders can summon after seeing nothing, while an attacker's units have to sit there a round in plain view.
- **Leyline + attack in one round:** I moved Imps by Leyline, then attacked with them in the same round. This was strong; it gave me two conquests. Worth the price.
- **Scorch:** 3 mana to remove a lone attacking Imp was worth it. In the last round I paid 54 mana to burn 6 Golems, which cancelled their attack. Leftover mana is worth nothing at the end, so this was a free win.

Confusing or surprising:
- Scorch can't target a tile that is empty now ("nothing to burn"), even though the rules say spells "see the round as it will be". That only holds for your own summons, not for a rival's.
- Leyline was refused for units that a move would bring to the source tile ("aren't 8 Imps"), even though moves happen first. Leyline on units summoned that round does work.
- I used only Imps. Golems give more power per mana, but their upkeep and Scorch's "weakest first" made me unsure they pay off. Seat 3's 6 Golems cost 6 upkeep a round, and my Scorch then burned them all.
