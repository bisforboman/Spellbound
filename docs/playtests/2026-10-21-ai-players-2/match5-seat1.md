# Match 5, Seat 1 (Trait-chaser)

Trait: Spellweaver, picked over Hearthkeeper because it depends only on my own choices. Final scores: Seat 2 21, Seat 1 19, Seat 3 18.

What I did: I cast four different spells by round 4: Blight R1, Lure R2, Ward R3, Scorch R4. That gave the full 3 trait points early. Lure on (0,-1) plus 3 Imps made a cheap monster clear, and Seat 3's own Lure on the same tile helped too. Scorch at 6 mana for 2 Imps was the worst buy, since Seat 3 used the rest of the stack the same round anyway. Blight (4) only took 2 mana off Seat 2. Ward was the spell I came back to; it held tiles and deterred attacks.

Strong: big Imp stacks (9, then 15) and late Golem walls. Ward on the tile you defend. After round 4 the trait no longer shaped my play, so it stopped steering anything.

Mistakes and confusion: in R6 I attacked an empty rival tile and forgot that summons resolve before attacks, so Seat 3's 15 fresh Imps (plus Ward) wiped out my force. Twice I left tiles without units and lost them to single attacks. No refusals happened. Turn order and the check previews were clear.
