# Match 3, Seat 2 (Spellcaster, trait Wanderer)

Final scores: Seat 1 27 (Warbrand), Seat 3 22 (Hexer), Seat 2 9. My Wanderer trait scored 0.

What I tried: I bid cheaply in the auction (2 and 1 mana, then passed for a free tile) to save mana for spells. I cast a spell in all 8 rounds and used all five: blight, ward (3 times), lure (twice), scorch and leyline.

Worth it:
- Ward plus 9 Imps beat Seat 1's 8-Imp attack in round 2. That was my best moment.
- Lure is cheap, but in round 5 I lured (-1,0) down to 1 and Seat 1 cleared it before I could. Lure helps whoever attacks first.

Not worth it:
- Scorching 1 Imp for 3 mana changed nothing.
- My last-round leyline split my Imps, so neither tile could hold.
- Blight (4 mana for about 2 mana of rival income) is poor value.

What felt off:
- Paying for a spell every round kept my army tiny. Seat 1's 44+ mana and leylined Imp stacks took a tile from me almost every round.
- Seat 1 spent 54 mana to scorch 6 Golems. That looks very strong for a rich player.
- Wanderer is hard. It needs separate groups, but you can only attune tiles next to your own, and your tiles keep getting taken.
- Seat 3 blighted twice, yet Hexer counted "2 Scorches and Blights".

No refusals.
