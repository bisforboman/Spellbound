# Match 1, Seat 1: Spellcaster

Personality: Spellcaster. Trait: Spellweaver. Final scores: Seat 2 got 26 (Hexer, winner), Seat 1 got 23, Seat 3 got 13.

Seat 1 cast a spell in all 8 rounds and used all five spells. That maxed the trait (+3) by round 4.
- **Lure** (3) plus 3 Imps cleared the power-3 monster tile and kept 1 Imp. It was the best value: a tile and a point.
- **Blight** (4, three times) cost a rival only 1-2 mana each time. That is weak for the price.
- **Leyline** (2) moved 1 Imp with no real purpose. It was only worth it for the trait.
- **Ward** (3, twice) was never tested in my own fights. It only adds +3, which is pointless against a 16-Imp stack.
- **Scorch** (9) burned 1 Golem, so my 9 Imps tied Seat 3's attack and I kept the tile. It was pricey but it decided the fight.

What felt strong: Seat 2's single 16-Imp stack, which took an empty tile every round. Defending one guessed tile is hopeless when they can choose among five. Seat 1 never built an army and lost 2 tiles at the end.

What surprised me: in round 6 Seat 2 scorched my lone Imp before my attack. Spells resolve before attacks, so my attack "had no units left to send." The rules state this order, but I didn't think it through. I also lucked into a free Steady tile when the top bids tied in the opening.
