# Match 4, Seat 2: Rules-lawyer, Hexer

Final scores: Seat 2 25 (winner), Seat 1 16, Seat 3 8.

What Seat 2 did: won a Shifting tile for 4, passed auction 2 on purpose to get a free leftover tile, won a Steady tile for 3, then attuned a tile every round I could. Late on I used Lure twice so a single Golem could clear each monster tile (3 vs 2).

**Loophole (likely a bug):** a Scorch with count 1 aimed at a tile holding only Golems burns nothing and costs 0, yet it was accepted and counted as a cast for Hexer. Seat 2 did this twice and got 2 trait points for 0 mana. A count-1 Scorch on an empty tile is refused ("nothing to burn"), which is inconsistent.

Spells: Blight (4) was fine. Scorching 6 Imps for 18 stopped an attack outright and was well worth it. Scorching 2 Golems for 18 in round 8 was poor value, because the 1 Golem left still took my empty tile. Both Lures (3 each) paid off.

Surprises: Auction 1 listed (1,-1) as "up for auction", but a bid on it was refused. Scorch with a count above what can burn is refused for its full price, even though you only pay for what burns. The check text repeats itself: "(guarded by power 2) (guarded by monsters, power 2)". A Golem that loses 2 power in a battle survives at full strength, which is unclear.
