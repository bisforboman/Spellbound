# Match 2, Seat 1: Hoarder, Warden

**Final scores:** Seat 1 31 (winner), Seat 2 27, Seat 3 15.

**What Seat 1 tried:** In the opening Seat 1 placed small bids, lost them all and got three free leftover tiles. Losing an auction cost nothing, so bidding felt nearly pointless. Seat 1 attuned a free neighbour in each of rounds 2 to 5 and summoned 4 Imps once. In rounds 6 and 7 those Imps took two empty Seat 3 tiles (2 conquest points). Seat 1 held all 3 opening tiles (Warden +2) and ended with 9 tiles.

**Spells:**
- **Scorch:** cast 4 times. Round 4 (2 Imps, 6 mana) stopped a real Seat 3 attack on an opening tile. Round 6 (a Golem, 9 mana) was fine. Round 8 (8 Imps, 24 mana) shut down Seat 2's last-round threat. In round 5 it cost 0 because the Golem moved away first, which is a nice rule.
- **Ward:** cast once (3 mana). It was never tested.

**What felt strong, pointless or confusing:** Scorch was the strongest defence. Banked mana scores nothing, which left Seat 1 with 66 mana unspent at the end, so hoarding has no payoff. Holding land and attuning early was strong.

**Misunderstandings and refusals:** Scorch's "count" is power, not units. A count of 1 on a Golem would burn nothing. One refusal surprised Seat 1: scorch needs a tile next to yours, so the 12 Imps one tile away could not be hit.
