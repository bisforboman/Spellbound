# Match 5, Seat 2 (Hoarder, trait Weaver)

Final: Seat 2 21, Seat 1 19, Seat 3 18. Seat 2 wins.

Plan: build one connected group of Steady and Shifting land for Weaver, and only fight when it was nearly free. Seat 2 lost one auction bid in a tie but got the tile free anyway, which was a nice surprise. The first attune of (1,1) collided with Seat 3, so the 4 mana stayed unspent but the round gave nothing.

Units: one Golem against the monster tile (-1,2) (a tie takes it): cheap and good for land plus a point. Two Golems on (-2,0) beat Seat 3's 4-Imp raid. In round 7 one Golem took Seat 1's empty (-1,1). In the last round 6 Golems plus Ward stopped 13 Imps, which stopped Seat 3 from overtaking Seat 2.

Spells: Ward three times. The one in round 3 turned out unneeded because nobody attacked; the one in round 8 was insurance. Seat 2 cast no other spells.

Strong: Golems on defence and the tie rule against monsters. Weaver stopped at 7 tiles (2 points); 9 tiles felt out of reach. Seat 2 ended with 27 unspent mana, which only counts as a tie-breaker, so it was hoarded to no purpose. Seat 1 was a Spellweaver, so its Blight in round 1 was part of its plan.
