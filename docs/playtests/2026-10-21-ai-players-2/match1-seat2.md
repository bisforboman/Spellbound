# Match 1, Seat 2 (Warlord, trait Hexer)

Final scores: Seat 2 26 (winner), Seat 1 23, Seat 3 13.

What I tried: I bought two cheap central tiles in the auction (2 and 3 mana) and passed the third to get a free one. After that it was one big stack of Imps: 6 Imps in round 1, then 13 more in round 4, so 16. That stack attacked an empty rival tile every round from round 2 on and took 6 tiles in all. Imps at 1 mana per power with no upkeep beat Golems over a short game.

Spells: Blight twice (4 each). It was worth it only for Hexer. Scorch twice: once on a 1-Imp tile for 3 mana, which was fine. Ward three times (3 each), and it was strong. In round 7 it tied a lone Golem with no units of mine on the tile, so I kept the tile.

Surprises:
- A Scorch whose target had left burned nothing and cost 0 mana, yet it counted toward Hexer (4 casts). That makes Hexer nearly free and looks like an exploit.
- Before acting, Scorch on an empty tile is refused, so you can't aim it at defenders a rival might summon that round.
- The AIs rarely kept defenders home. An undefended tile next to a stack was free to take almost every round, so attacking was overwhelmingly strong.
- Seat 3's Golems ate its income in upkeep.
