# Combo player (match 2, Seat 2)
**Final scores and my trait** Seat 2 27, Seat 3 18, Seat 1 8. Seat 2 won as Hexer (3 points from 5 Scorches and Blights).

**What I enjoyed**
- The Sanctum + Hexer engine. I built the Sanctum on round 1, and from then on every Blight, Ward and Scorch near it cost 1 less. It was the one moment the game felt like a deck coming together.
- Scorch + attack on the same turn. On round 3 I burned a Golem, then walked 4 Imps onto (0,0) for 2 conquest points. Doing spells before attacks gives a real sequencing puzzle.
- Scorch as a pre-emptive counter. Twice I burned Seat 1's Imps before they could attack me ("has no units left to send"). That was a satisfying surprise.
- Simultaneous play made reads matter. Seat 3 warded the empty (1,-1) and I still took it with 1 Imp left over.

**What bored or frustrated me**
- The Sanctum's discount is 1 per cast, not per power, so an 8-mana Scorch became 7. Its effect is flat, not something that grows.
- Hexer was finished by round 3 (three casts). After that the trait gave me no reason to build further.
- Attune costs 1 + 3 per tile held, so after round 1 it was never worth it. Growth came only from fighting.
- My 2 Golems and Seat 3's 2 Golems both attacked the empty (-1,1) and wiped each other out. That was 4 mana and 2 upkeep for nothing, and nobody could have seen it coming.
- By rounds 7 and 8 I had 20+ mana and nothing interesting to spend it on except end-game upgrades.

**Did building and fighting feel worth it?**
Upgrades were worth it. Each one is 1 point at the end, and the Sanctum paid back its 5 mana in discounts and set up my whole plan. The Towers were bought just for points; their effect never mattered. Attacking paid well: 2 points per tile plus the tile's income, and it was my main way to grow. The best fights were the ones where a spell changed the maths first. Plain unit-against-unit fights felt like dice I couldn't read.

**Compared with the games I love, what's missing**
Dominion and Magic reward stacking engines: each piece makes the next one stronger. Here every upgrade and spell is a single +1 that stands alone. Spirit Island gives each spirit unique powers and rising tracks. Here traits are just end-game scoring counters, with nothing to unlock along the way.

**Top 3 additions I'd want**
1. Upgrades that combine (Dominion): a Shrine next to a Sanctum gives a free Ward each round, or two Towers next to each other share their defence.
2. Trait powers that grow (Spirit Island): Hexer's third cast unlocks something, such as Blight also burning 1 power, instead of the trait simply capping out.
3. Responses or tricks that trigger on a condition (Magic instants): a spell that goes off only if the tile is attacked, to punish the empty-tile rushes.

**Would I play again?** 3/5. The Sanctum-Scorch turn was really fun, and resolving everything at once makes for good surprises. But the engine stops growing by mid-game, and the last rounds were only about spending mana on points.
