# 4X fan (match 1, Seat 2)

**Final scores and my trait**
Seat 2 31 (won on the tile tiebreak, 6 tiles to 4), Seat 3 31, Seat 1 27. My trait was Weaver, and it scored only 1 because Seat 3 kept cutting my group in half.

**What I enjoyed**
- The Unveils in rounds 2 and 4. Dark tiles turning into real choices felt like exploring, and (0,0) and (0,1) showing up right between my tiles made a natural expansion path.
- The opening auction tie. Seat 1 and I both bid 2 on the Wild 4, nobody won it, and then I got it free as my leftover tile. That was a nice surprise.
- Rally, Leyline and reinforcements together make a satisfying combo. In round 7 I leylined 2 Golems in, moved a third, attacked, and guarded the home tile, all in one order, with `check` showing the result first.
- Points are banked every round, so the land I took early kept paying.

**What bored or frustrated me**
- Attuning gets steep fast (4, 7, 10, 13). After round 4 I stopped growing on purpose, and the empire was as big as it would ever get.
- I never built a single upgrade. There is a hard choice of one stance per round, and land and defence always beat a Shrine. So the economy never grew, and I ended with 22 mana I had no use for.
- From round 5 on, the game turned into Imps with Rally taking undefended tiles back and forth. My (0,-1) and (0,0) swapped owners three times, which felt like trench warfare, not empire-building.
- The map has 19 tiles, so there was hardly any frontier. By round 4 nothing worth having was left.

**Did reading my rivals matter?**
A little. I read Seat 1's four-Golem opening correctly as Golemwright, which was worth the point that won the game. I misread Seat 3's raids as Warbrand when it was Beastbane. Fog of war made me guess where Imps would strike, and I usually guessed wrong.

**Did I have enough to do each round, and did my mana have a use?**
For the first four rounds, yes: each round had a clear expansion target. After that the decision was mostly "which tile do I retake," and mana piled up. One stance per round is the bottleneck. Mana was rarely what limited me.

**Did building and fighting feel worth it?**
Fighting did: taking a defended tile gives 2 points, and Rally makes attacks dependable. Building did not. A 5-mana Shrine with 4 rounds left gives about 4 mana and 1 point, and it costs the stance I needed for land or units. I never felt pulled to build.

**Compared with the games I love, what's missing**
A long arc. Round 8 played just like round 5. There was no tech, no unit getting better, no economy compounding, and no new kinds of actions unlocking later.

**Top 3 additions I'd want**
1. A small tech track: spend mana to unlock stronger Golems or cheaper attunes, like the Eclipse tech board.
2. Building as a free side action, the way Civilization cities build while you move armies, so upgrades don't compete with the stance.
3. A bigger, staged map with more Unveils and monster-guarded prizes, so exploring lasts past round 4, like Twilight Imperium's wider galaxy.

**Would I play again?** 3/5. It is quick and tense, and the opening and Unveils are good. But the middle and end feel flat, and spare mana with nothing to grow into is exactly what bores a 4X player.
