# Wargamer (match 1, Seat 1)

**Final scores and my trait**
Seat 2 31 (Weaver, won on tiles), Seat 3 31 (Beastbane), Seat 1 27 (Golemwright, 4 Golems for 1 point; both guesses wrong).

**What I enjoyed**
- From round 2 on there was a battle every round, usually several. Five of my eight rounds were attacks, and I took a tile in five of them (including the only monster tile).
- The back-and-forth over the Wild 4 at (-2,1): Seat 3 took it, Seat 1 took it back with a rallied double-Golem strike. That felt like Kemet.
- Rally plus a reinforcement on the tile I left behind is a neat attack-and-hold package. `check` showing the expected battle result made a bold move easy to commit to.
- Conquest points banked at once, never lost. Fighting always paid something.

**What bored or frustrated me**
- My first real attack (3 Golems into an empty tile) ran into 4 summoned Golems plus a Ward: 9 against 15, wiped out. Simultaneous orders make an empty tile a trap, and there was no way to scout or back out.
- The board turned into a carousel. Everyone, me included, hit whichever neighbour tile was empty, so tiles changed hands round after round while armies rarely met. Few battles were big.
- Golem upkeep made round 2 painful (3 mana income, 4 upkeep). Golemwright pulls against fighting: Golems I send out die, and 9 Golems for 3 points never looked reachable.
- My attackers left home undefended every time, and I lost a tile almost every round to 3 rallied Imps.

**Did reading my rivals matter?**
Mostly for defence: Seat 3 reinforced and rallied the same way every round, and the pattern was readable. My trait guesses failed (Warbrand was really Beastbane, Hearthkeeper was really Weaver), because behaviour like "attacks a lot" fits several traits.

**Did I have enough to do each round, and did my mana have a use?**
Yes. The usual 6 mana went on Rally plus one reinforcing Golem, and I always had a target. I never used build, attune or scorch, and I ended with 7 mana unspent. Reinforcements are capped at 3 power, so extra mana couldn't buy a bigger army in an attack round.

**Did building and fighting feel worth it?**
Fighting did: conquest gave about 9 of my 27 points. Building never looked worth 5 mana in an 8-round game, because a Tower only defends with 2 more and a Shrine pays back too slowly.

**Compared with the games I love, what's missing**
Massed armies and decisive battles. Most fights were 1 to 3 units against an empty tile. There's no momentum (in Blood Rage, losing still feeds you), no army that sweeps across the map, and only one monster tile to fight.

**Top 3 additions I'd want**
1. Kemet-style reward for the defender or loser (a point for a held defence, or units refunded), so stacking up and meeting armies head-on beats hitting empty tiles.
2. Blood Rage-style big, contested monster or region prizes in the middle of the map, so all three players pile into one fight.
3. A Risk-style "march" order: one stance that both summons and attacks with the new units, so a mana-rich turn can become a real offensive.

**Would I play again?** 4/5. It has constant conflict and quick, readable battles. But the fights are skirmishes over empty tiles rather than clashes of armies, and the Golem trait works against the fighting it should reward.
