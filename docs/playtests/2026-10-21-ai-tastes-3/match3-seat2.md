# Combo player (match 3, Seat 2)

**Final scores and my trait** Seat 2 won with 37, Seat 1 had 30 (Beastbane) and Seat 3 had 22 (Wanderer). My trait was Warden and I kept all 3 opening tiles, which was worth 2 points.

**What I enjoyed**
- Spells "seeing the round as it will be" is the best idea here. Lure in round 2 dropped a monster to power 2, and in round 4 a single Golem took it and survived. That was a two-turn plan that actually came together.
- Rally plus a lone Golem is a neat interaction. The rally power takes the blows first, and a loss of 2 can't kill a power-3 Golem, so one Golem clears a power-3 monster with no losses. Leyline then an attack in the same round (round 6) felt like a real trick.
- Rounds resolve together and the report is short and readable. `check` showing the exact outcome made plans easy to test.

**What bored or frustrated me**
- By round 5 the right move was always the same: find an empty enemy tile next to a Golem, walk in, take 2 points. Rounds 6 to 8 were all copies of that turn.
- I ended with 42 mana unspent. With one stance and at most one spell a round, my income outgrew anything I could do with it. That ceiling kills the engine fantasy.
- Upgrades don't combine. I never built the Sanctum because one spell a round makes "spells cost 1 less" worth about 1 mana.
- Guessing traits was a blind lottery. I couldn't find out which trait names exist, so I made up "Hexer" and "Weaver" and both were wrong.

**Did reading my rivals matter?** Only a little. Seat 3's 4 Golems appeared next to my opening tile in round 2, so I spent round 3 on 4 Golems plus Ward. They never attacked, and I can't tell whether my defence put them off or they had other plans. After that their tiles were plainly empty and I just took them. I never outguessed anyone. Nobody could guess my trait from what I did, except Seat 3, which got Warden right, probably from the small list that was offered.

**Did building and fighting feel worth it?** Fighting, yes: each clean capture banked 2 points, and that decided the game. Building, barely. The early Shrine paid back and was worth a point at the end, but each later build cost a whole stance that could have been a capture. Golem upkeep was fine.

**Compared with the games I love, what's missing** Engine growth. In Dominion and Spirit Island your turn gets bigger as you build. Here my turn stayed the same size, one stance and one spell, from round 1 to round 8, so the mana piled up with nothing to spend it on.

**Top 3 additions I'd want**
1. Upgrades that grant actions, like Dominion's "+1 Action": for example, a Sanctum that lets you cast a second spell.
2. Spell combos with named payoffs, like Spirit Island's element thresholds: Blight then Scorch on the same tile does more.
3. A public pool of the traits that could be in play, like Magic's open card pool, so guessing is a deduction and not a coin flip.

**Would I play again?** 3/5. The Lure-to-Rally setup was a real combo moment, but the second half was a solved loop, and a growing pile of mana with nothing to spend it on is the opposite of a build coming together.
