# 4X fan (match 2, Seat 1)

**Final scores and my trait** Seat 1 38, Seat 3 20, Seat 2 15. I was Golemwright and ended with 9 Golems for the full 3 points.

**What I enjoyed**
- The Unveils. Dark hexes turning into Steady or Wild tiles, plus a guarded monster tile, gave me a small taste of the "what's out there" feeling.
- The monster tile felt like a proper 4X objective: Lure it down to 2, then clear it with one Golem that survives. Planning that over two rounds was the best moment of the match.
- Seat 1 grew from 2 tiles to 9 and income from 6 to 15. That arc was real.
- Golem casualties are easy to predict ("losing 2 can't kill a Golem"), so I could plan battles, and `check` told me the outcome before I committed.

**What bored or frustrated me**
- One stance a round is a hard cap on growth. By round 7 Seat 1 had 37 mana and could still only do one thing. I finished with 30 mana unspent. The economy grows but my ability to act doesn't, so the late game feels like the early game with bigger numbers.
- Attune gets very expensive (10 mana for a 6th tile). Taking empty rival tiles with a Rally (2 points plus the tile) was so much better that I stopped exploring and just raided.
- Seat 1 never built anything. There is no tech, and the upgrades are three one-off flat bonuses.
- After round 4 the map was fully revealed, so the exploring was over by mid-game.

**Did reading my rivals matter?** Barely. I could see that the rival tiles next to mine were empty, and that was the only read I needed: if a tile sits empty, raid it. I had no idea what traits were in play beyond the names in the rules, so my one guess (Seat 2 as Weaver) was a blind stab and it was wrong. The only time I outguessed anyone was the final-round Ward on (-1,0), which tied Seat 2's Golem attack and saved a tile. That didn't decide anything.

**Did building and fighting feel worth it?** Fighting, very much. Four conquests made up about a third of my score and nobody defended. Building didn't feel worth it to Seat 1: 5 mana and a whole stance for +1 yield over a few rounds lost to a 2-point raid every time. Seat 3 got 3 end points from upgrades, but it lost twice as many tiles.

**Compared with the games I love, what's missing** A tech tree or anything that changes what I *can do* over time, a map big enough to keep exploring, and more actions as an empire grows. In Civ or Eclipse, round 8 plays nothing like round 1. Here only the numbers change.

**Top 3 additions I'd want**
1. Extra actions earned through growth, like Eclipse's influence discs or TI's command tokens: a second stance at 6+ tiles, or one bought with mana.
2. A small tech track like Eclipse's research: upgrades that unlock new unit types or let you attune two hexes away.
3. Map edges that keep unfolding with ruins or relics, like Civ's goody huts, so exploring lasts past round 4.

**Would I play again?** 3/5. The match is fast, the battles are easy to read and the monster clear felt good. But growth hits the one-stance ceiling, and the mana I couldn't spend made the empire feel hollow at its peak.
