# Wargamer (match 1, Seat 1)

**Final scores and my trait**
Seat 1 39 (won), Seat 2 28, Seat 3 17. Seat 1 was Weaver (largest connected group): 8 tiles, full 3 points, plus 1 for guessing Seat 3 was a Hexer.

**What I enjoyed**
- Golems feel like an army. Three of them in round 2 carried me to four conquests in rounds 3-8, worth 2 points each, and every tile I took grew my Weaver group.
- Rally plus Leyline let me take a towered tile in one go: I moved two Golems across my land and hit (-1,0) at 9 power against its 2.
- Seat 2 taking (-1,0) with Scorch plus a Golem stack, and Seat 1 taking it back with 17 power the next round, was the best moment of the game. It was a real back-and-forth over one tile.
- Points banked every round meant pressing the attack always paid. Nothing ever told me to sit and build.

**What bored or frustrated me**
- Nearly every attack hit an empty tile: 8 against 0, 17 against 2. Units that attack leave their tile undefended, so everyone raided whatever the others had just emptied. It played out like three armies passing each other, and two stacks never met head on.
- One stance a round meant summoning cost me a whole round of fighting. Round 6 was spent shoring up the defence, and I still lost a tile.
- Rally on every attack felt like a tax, not a choice. In the last two rounds all three seats cast it.
- Monsters at power 3 were never worth a detour once rival tiles stood empty next door.

**Did reading my rivals matter?**
A little. I couldn't see units more than one tile from my land, and I had to guess where Seat 2's 4 Golems would strike. I guessed wrong: I warded (-1,1), and they scorched and took (-1,0). My guess that Seat 3 was a Hexer came from their Blight in round 1 and was worth 1 point. Outguessing mostly meant guessing which tile a rival had left empty, and that decided a lot of points. It never decided a big battle.

**Did building and fighting feel worth it?**
Fighting did: about half my points came from conquests and the land and upgrades I took with them. Building did not, for me. Seat 3 built two Towers and lost both tiles, and I ended up holding both Towers for 2 points. A Tower on an empty tile only slows an attack a little.

**Compared with the games I love, what's missing**
Pitched battles and reasons to stay home. In Risk and Blood Rage armies collide because the ground matters and you can see the threat. Here, moving out and attacking was almost always better than defending, so the fights were raids.

**Top 3 additions I'd want**
1. Blood Rage-style glory for losing a battle (or a point for every unit killed), so a defended clash pays both sides, not just raids on empty tiles.
2. Kemet-style attack plus recruit in one turn (a small summon alongside an attack), so building an army doesn't cost a round of war.
3. Risk-style continent bonus: a contested central tile or region worth points every round, which would pull the stacks together.

**Would I play again?** 4/5. It's fast and aggressive and rewards bold moves. It would be a 5 if battles were actual collisions and not mostly raids on empty tiles.
