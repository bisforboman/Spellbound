# 4X fan (match 1, Seat 2)
**Final scores and my trait** Seat 2 30, Seat 1 29, Seat 3 13. I won by one point, and that point came from correctly guessing Seat 1 was Warbrand. My trait was Weaver, and it paid only 1 because my group ended at 5 tiles.

**What I enjoyed**
- The Unveils. Dark hexes turning into a yield-3 tile next to my two corner tiles felt like exploring. Taking (2,-1) to join my pieces was my favourite move.
- Lure followed by a Golem to clear monsters was a small but real plan: weaken over two turns, then strike. It gave me 4 conquest points and my two best tiles.
- My income grew from 7 to 17 mana a round, so there was a visible arc from small to big.
- Rally swung the last round. `check` showing the exact battle outcome before committing is excellent.

**What bored or frustrated me**
- I ended with 56 unspent mana. My economy grew but had nothing to grow into. One stance a round meant I could never attack and build in the same turn, so Shrines and Towers never happened.
- Attune cost climbed to 13–16 mana (3 per tile beyond 3), so expanding peacefully stopped after round 2. Every later tile came from a fight.
- The map is tiny: 19 hexes with 3 players. There was no frontier left to push into.
- Ward did nothing when my Golem marched out of (1,-2), and Seat 1 walked in. That was my mistake, but the lesson was "never leave a tile", which is a defensive habit, not a fun one.

**Did reading my rivals matter?** Yes, literally: the Warbrand guess decided the match. Seat 1's constant conquest made it easy to read. Seat 3's Golemwright was invisible because it had no Golems.

**Did you follow rivals' stances, and did it change your choices?** I put a follow-Attune or follow-Summon on almost every round. None ever fired, because nobody else attuned after round 2. Guessing stances mattered more for collisions: Seat 3 and I bounced off (0,2) in round 1 and then traded units on (0,-1) in round 7.

**Did building and fighting feel worth it?** Fighting yes: monster clears and the final counterattack gave most of my points. Building never got a turn. With 5 mana I'd have loved a Shrine, but each turn spent building was a turn I didn't take a tile.

**Compared with the games I love, what's missing** No tech or upgrade tree, so round 8 played like round 1 with a bigger bank. No place to sink a big economy. No map beyond the starting rim. No second action once you're rich.

**Top 3 additions I'd want**
1. A mana sink with lasting tiers, like Eclipse's tech track: spend surplus on permanent upgrades (cheaper attune, +1 Golem power).
2. A second action at higher income, like Civilization's growing production: e.g. at 15+ income you can build alongside any stance.
3. A wider frontier that unveils further out, like Twilight Imperium's outer systems, so expanding peacefully stays possible late.

**Would I play again?** 3/5. It's tight and readable, and the finish was thrilling. But my empire hit a ceiling by round 5 and I finished sitting on unused wealth.
