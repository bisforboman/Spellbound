# Casual player (match 2, Seat 2)
**Final scores and my trait**: Seat 1 41 (Warbrand), Seat 3 24 (Golemwright), Seat 2 16 (Hearthkeeper, 2 of 3 Steady tiles kept at the end, plus 1 right guess).

**What I enjoyed**
- Hearthkeeper was a goal I understood in seconds ("hold Steady tiles"), just like a Ticket to Ride ticket.
- Round 5 was my high point: 3 Golems plus a Ward held off a rallied attack 12 against 11. Seeing "the attackers are wiped out" was exactly the moment each turn should have.
- Taking back (2,-1) with one Golem in round 6 felt good, and the report showed the point I got for it.
- `check` is great. It gives the whole cost in one line ("All together: 10 mana, leaving 2"), so I didn't have to do the sums.

**What bored or frustrated me**
- Two of my first three choices were ties that nobody won. In the auction and again in round 1, Seat 2 lost the tile because someone picked the same one. Each time Seat 2 lost a turn for nothing, and in the auction Seat 1 then got that tile free.
- Seat 1 snowballed. They took (1,0) together with the Tower I had just paid 5 for, then (2,-1) twice, then (2,-2) in the last round. Every round I was only defending.
- The last round felt unfair. Seat 1 spent 27 mana on Scorch to burn all three of my Golems, and my Ward plus 3 Golems ended up as "power 12 against 3". I had no way to see that coming or to answer it.
- Coordinates like "(2,-1)" and the neighbour rule. Without a picture I kept working out hex maths, which Catan never asks of me.

**Did reading my rivals matter?** Somewhat. Seat 1 attacked every round with Rally, so I guessed Warbrand and got it right. Knowing it still didn't help much, because their scorch-and-rally attacks outgrew anything Seat 2 could spend on defence.

**Did you follow rivals' stances, and did it change your choices?** I added a follow order three times (summon twice, once with a build), and none of them happened: nobody else picked Summon those rounds. I couldn't follow Attune while attuning myself, which confused me at first. Guessing stances felt like a coin flip, not a decision I could read from the board.

**Did building and fighting feel worth it?** Fighting yes, once: the round-5 defence was the best moment of the game. Building no: my Tower was captured the next round and it scored for Seat 1. Golem upkeep also ate my income near the end.

**Compared with the games I love, what's missing**
A picture of the board, a way to catch up when someone runs away with the lead, and trading or any friendly contact between players. In Catan, a leader gets the robber and nobody trades with them. Here nothing slowed Seat 1 down.

**Top 3 additions I'd want**
1. A drawn hex map with seat colours, like a Carcassonne table. I want to see my land, not read a list of coordinates.
2. A brake on the leader, like Catan's robber or not trading with the leader: for example, attacks on the last-place player cost more.
3. Settle ties instead of voiding them (earlier bid wins, or a die roll), the way Ticket to Ride simply gives a route to whoever claims it first.

**Would I play again?** 2/5. The turns are quick and the defence moment was fun, but I lost a tile almost every round and the 27-mana Scorch made my careful defence pointless.
