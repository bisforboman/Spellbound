# Wargamer (match 1, Seat 2)

**Final scores and my trait**
Seat 1 28 (Hearthkeeper, winner), me 20, Seat 3 20. My trait was Beastbane (points for clearing monsters). Both unveils in my game revealed zero monster tiles, so it scored 0. I picked it over Golemwright and never had a chance to use it.

**What I enjoyed**
I went to war in every round I could and took three tiles. The round-6 play was the best moment: I scorched Seat 1's 4 visible Imps for 12 mana and walked in with 3 Imps. Simultaneous orders make each attack a real bluff. Seat 1 read my round-5 strike and summoned 14 Imps exactly where I hit, and that hurt in a good way. Battle maths is quick and readable, and a tie wiping out both sides feels brutal and fair.

**What bored or frustrated me**
- The auction dropped me on the map edge, boxed in with no free tile next to me, so from round 1 my only way to grow was attacking.
- An empty tile defends almost as well as a garrisoned one, because the defender can summon 14 Imps there before the attack lands. There's no point in "positioning" an army.
- The turtle won. Seat 1 sat on 34 unspent mana, and Hearthkeeper paid 3 points for owning Steady tiles. Fighting cost me units and mana for +1 point per capture.
- Golem upkeep makes Imps strictly better for strikes, so the bigger unit felt pointless.
- No monsters ever appeared, so I had no PvE target.

**Compared with the games I love, what's missing**
Blood Rage gives glory for losing battles and rewards being in the fight. Here a failed attack is pure loss. Kemet hands out victory points for winning attacks and lets you recruit only in your own cities, which forces you to commit forces before the fight. Risk has card sets and continent bonuses that pay off big for snowballing. Here conquest is worth +1 point, the same as hoarding 10 mana.

**Top 3 additions I'd want**
1. Summons only on tiles without adjacent enemies, or only on a home tile, so you can't reinforce instantly (Kemet's recruitment limits). Armies would have to be placed in advance, and that makes reads matter.
2. Scoring for attacking, win or lose, like glory for the losers (Blood Rage), so a bold player isn't punished for fighting.
3. Region or continent bonuses for holding a connected cluster (Risk), so big pushes pay off big.

**Would I play again?** 3/5. The bluffing in combat is tense and fast, but the scoring pays the player who doesn't fight.
