# Bluffer (match 3, Seat 1)

**Final scores and my trait**
Seat 2 (Wildheart) 29, me (Beastbane) 20, Seat 3 (Weaver) 12. Beastbane paid out only 1 of its 3 points. The board had just one monster tile, and I cleared it in round 3 with Lure and a Golem.

**What I enjoyed**
- Simultaneous secret orders are a good base for reading people. Going up against Seat 3 in round 5, I read its 7 Imps as aimed at me and summoned 12 Imps. Its attack bounced off, and that felt great.
- Scorch is a real threat. Seat 2 kept a pile of 40 mana and burned stacks without warning. You know a huge bank could hit you anywhere, but you never know where.
- When two players attune the same tile, nobody gets it and nobody pays. It's a small game of chicken I liked, and opening bids work the same way.

**What bored or frustrated me**
- My one big gamble lost 11 to 12 against a summon plus a Ward. Defenders can add both after seeing the threat, so attacking felt like guessing with the odds against me, not outguessing anyone.
- Nothing I said or did could shift anyone else's choices. There's no table talk, no deals and no way to bluff on purpose. I could only guess.
- Hoarding mana and sitting still beat fighting. The leader rarely attacked, kept a big bank and won on land, mana and Wild tiles.
- Traits are secret during the opening and revealed when the rounds begin, so there's nothing left to deduce once real play starts.

**Compared with the games I love, what's missing**
Diplomacy has binding-free negotiation and backstabs. Cosmic Encounter has alliance invitations and face-down cards played into a fight. Coup lets you claim a role and get called out. Here every rival just commits and waits. Nobody can commit before a battle in the open and then betray it.

**Top 3 additions I'd want**
1. A public "pact" or "threat" line each round that isn't binding: deals and betrayals (Diplomacy).
2. Face-down combat cards or hidden reinforcements committed by both sides, so fights hinge on bluffing (Cosmic Encounter).
3. Traits stay secret until scored, and you can challenge a guess for a reward or penalty (Coup).

**Would I play again?**
3/5. Secret simultaneous orders give it real tension. Without communication or bluff tools, though, my reads only work as guesses.
