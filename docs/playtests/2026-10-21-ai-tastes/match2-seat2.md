# Combo player (match 2, Seat 2)

**Final scores and my trait**
Seat 3 (Wildheart) 24, Seat 1 (Warden) 23, me (Hexer) 22. I led or tied from round 1 to round 6, and lost on the end-of-game mana and trait bonuses. My Hexer goal was full by round 5 (three Blights and a Scorch), so my last four spells counted for nothing.

**What I enjoyed**
- Lure plus Golem was my favourite moment. Lure dropped the monster to 2, so my Golem won 3 against 2 and survived, because a loss of 2 can't kill a power-3 unit. That made a mana spell into a unit that kept going. A small combo, but a real one.
- Spells resolve before attacks, so I could Scorch first and then attack. You plan the sum of power, not just send units.
- Choosing a secret trait in the opening, and the opening auction that ends in a tie, both make for good reveals.

**What bored or frustrated me**
- Mid-game was musical chairs. Units left to attack, empty tiles changed hands, and points went round in a circle. My Scorch burned 2 Imps, and the other 2 had already walked off to take my tile.
- You get one stance and one spell a round, so nothing builds. In round 7 I had a lot of mana and only one good thing to do with it.
- The five spells are flat effects. None of them gets stronger with another one, with your trait, or over time.
- Hexer is done after three casts. The goal stops shaping my turns.

**Compared with the games I love, what's missing**
- Dominion: there's no engine. Nothing I buy in round 2 makes round 6 better, apart from more tiles.
- Magic: there are no triggered abilities and no instant-speed reply.
- Spirit Island: there are no elemental thresholds, so playing a set of spells unlocks nothing.

**Top 3 additions I'd want**
1. **Permanent upgrades you buy for a tile** (a Ley Shrine that makes spells cast from next to it cheaper). Building has to pay off later. From Dominion, its action and economy cards.
2. **Thresholds on traits**: after your 2nd Blight, your Blights also take 1 mana from the rival. That turns a trait that just counts into a build that grows. From Spirit Island, its element thresholds.
3. **One reaction card per round** (a hidden ward that triggers when you're attacked). It makes interactions you can't see coming. From Magic: instants, and Dominion: Moat.

**Would I play again?** 3/5. The rules are tight and quick, and the Lure-Golem play hints at more. But my best play was the same in round 2 and round 8, and I want a game where my turns change as it goes on.
