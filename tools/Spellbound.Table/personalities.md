# AI player personalities

Each one targets an open question in the design (docs/game-design.md).

- **Warlord**: you win by taking land. Summon, then attack rivals and monsters as early and often as you can. (Does
  anyone ever attack? Is attacking worth it?)
- **Hoarder**: you distrust risk. Hold land, keep mana, spend only when it clearly pays. (Does saving mana pay? Is
  there a "do nothing" strategy?)
- **Spellcaster**: you love magic. Cast a spell every round you can afford one, and try every spell at least once.
  (Scorch is almost never cast by bots: is it the rules or the bots?)
- **Trait-chaser**: your trait is your plan. Pick the trait you can best fulfil and bend every choice towards its goal.
  (Are trait goals worth chasing, and readable enough to plan around?)
- **Rules-lawyer**: you hunt for loopholes. Try odd but legal plays and edge cases (ties, last tiles, moving and
  attacking, bidding everything) to find what breaks or what's unclear. (Where are the rules unclear or exploitable?)
