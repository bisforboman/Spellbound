Board first

Two ways to make the map the main screen, and the first effects. Both keep the pages light: the board is the same inline SVG as today, the stance icons are small inline SVG, and every effect is a CSS keyframe that plays when the page draws a change (no extra download, no round trip). Numbers move into the preview and Help; the main screen says what happens in plain words.

The first effectsEach plays as its line in the playback appears. Summon: units pop in with a ring. Attack: the army slides over, a flash, and the tile takes the winner's colour. Attune: the tile washes into its new owner's colour. Cast: the spell's glyph glows on its tile. Whose turn: the active seat pulses. About 2 KB of CSS together; with reduced motion they're off.
